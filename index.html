🚀 COMING SOON

SquadHub 2.0

SquadHub 2.0 is planned as a major upgrade to the current SquadHub experience. The focus is on smarter verification, better squad management, communication, more games and deeper player interaction.

🤖

Advanced AI Tools

Improved AI-powered tools designed to help analyze task submissions, assist players and provide smarter SquadHub features.

🎮

More Games

SquadHub 2.0 is planned to support additional games and more game-specific challenges.

👥

Custom Squads

Create and organize custom squads with your own players, squad identity and team structure.

👑

Team Leader Election

Squad members will be able to participate in a planned team-leader election system.

🎯

Team Tasks

Future team-based challenges will allow multiple players to work together toward shared objectives.

💬

Conversations & Group Chats

Planned messaging features will allow players to communicate through conversations and group chats.

🎁

Google Play Redeem Codes & Promocodes

Google Play redeem codes and SquadHub promocodes are planned for SquadHub 2.0. They are intentionally not available in SquadHub 1.0.

🎮

Improved Game Controls & Integration

Future versions may provide improved game-related controls and deeper integrations where technically and officially supported.

🔐

New Verification System

A more advanced verification architecture is planned to make task verification more reliable and easier to manage.

🚧 Development notice: These are planned SquadHub 2.0 features. They are announcements only in SquadHub 1.0 and are not active through this prototype.
